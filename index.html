04 Before we price it

Eight questions that set the scope and the fee.

The retainer depends on responsibility, fidelity, frequency, timelines and ad hoc availability. These answers fix all five.

AreaQuestionWhy it matters
AudienceWho is Stereo Chai for, and where do they mostly live?Shapes every topic, language and posting time.
The musicWhich genres, languages, eras and artists should the page champion? New releases, catalogue, or both?Decides research depth and which buckets lead.
PillarsCan the content pillars, guidelines and templates be shared before we start?Lets the first plan land in week two.
FidelityShould most posts stay within the templates, or do you expect motion, illustration or stop motion regularly?The biggest driver of production time.
Frequency and mixWhere in the 3 to 5 range do you want to start, and what carousel-to-video split?Sets the monthly volume.
PlatformsNative versions for TikTok and Instagram, or one edit published to both?Changes editing and publishing load.
Footage and rightsWhere will video footage come from, how often are shoots expected, and who leads clearances?Defines who carries which responsibility.
Timelines and ad hocAre 48-hour turnarounds in business days, which time zone, and do you want same-day posts when news breaks? What response window on comments?Sets team availability and on-call cover.
